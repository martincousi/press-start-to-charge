Press Start to Charge

Online scheduling of home charging for electric vehicles (EVs), played as a game. Each block is one EV: its width is the charging time it needs, and it must be placed between the EV's arrival and departure. Blocks arrive one at a time, in order of arrival, and you do not see the next one. Keep the load as flat as possible over the 24 hours: the score is the difference between the highest and the lowest load (Max-Min, lower is better).

Keys: ←→ shift the block one slot (15 min) inside its window, ↓, Space or Enter drop it. After 96 shifts it drops by itself. 1-5 level, N new seed, R restart.

The block falls column by column: each slot it covers gains one unit of load. Red columns are outside the EV's window. A slot cannot exceed the capacity (top of the board); if an EV has no place left, the game ends. There is no undo: as in the online problem, a decision is final.

Benchmarks: Greedy starts each EV where the sum of loads under the block is smallest (the rule of Chau et al. 2023). Current practice starts charging on arrival.

Game from: Ghahtarani A, Cousineau M, Farahmand A-m, Mendoza JE (2026) Press Start to Charge: Gamifying the Online Centralized Charging Scheduling Problem. Under revision at INFORMS Journal on Computing. arXiv:2601.12543. Arrival patterns follow the paper's four scenarios (EC Appendix D); the warm-up level is not from the paper.
